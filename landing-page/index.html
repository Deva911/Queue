What it handles

The six questions your front desk answers all day.

Every conversation starts from the same short list of options. Customers tap one instead of typing, so they never get lost and your staff never repeat themselves.

Book an appointment

Customers pick a service and a preferred time. The request lands in your dashboard for staff to confirm or decline.

Services and prices

Your own list of services, durations and prices. Switch a service off and the bot stops offering it.

Reschedule

Customers ask to move an existing appointment without a phone call. Staff approve the new time.

Cancel

Cancellations come through in writing, with a record of who asked and when.

Payment information

Share how and when to pay. Secure payment links are on the roadmap, and a payment only shows as paid once the provider confirms it.

Talk to a person

The bot steps aside and the chat goes to your team's inbox. It only resumes when a staff member hands it back.

Customers can also type MENU BACK HELP or HUMAN at any point.
Who it's for

Set up for how your business books.

You add your own services, prices, opening hours and booking rules from a dashboard. No code, no scripts to write.

Built to be trusted

It never tells a customer something that isn't true.

Automated replies are only useful if people can rely on them. These rules are built into the product from the first version.

PENDING

Requests aren't bookings

A new request stays pending until your team confirms it. The bot never promises a slot it hasn't checked.

PAID

Paid means paid

A payment is only marked paid when the payment provider confirms it, never because a customer says so.

HUMAN

A person is always one word away

Customers can ask for a person from any step. Automation pauses until your staff hand the chat back.

PRIVATE

Your data stays yours

Each business gets its own workspace. One business can never see another's customers, chats or bookings.
